At CAPRA 2026, I discussed exponentially-convergent numerical simulations of circular-orbit EMRIs in black hole perturbation theory. The presentation focused on accuracy gains from discontinuous Galerkin-style discretizations and their role in robust gravitational-wave modeling pipelines.
