I presented recent progress on exponentially-convergent time-domain simulations for circular-orbit extreme-mass-ratio inspirals (EMRIs). The talk emphasized how high-order numerical methods improve waveform accuracy and efficiency for long-duration evolutions relevant to space-based gravitational-wave observations.
